Many people with tinnitus find it easier to live with when there is a soft sound around it. Two kinds are common: noise with a gap around your tone, and a narrow band of noise right on it. Find your tone, then try both.

This is for comfort, not treatment, and murmur is not a medical device. Keep the sound quieter than your tinnitus rather than drowning it. If tinnitus is new, one-sided or worsening, see a doctor.
